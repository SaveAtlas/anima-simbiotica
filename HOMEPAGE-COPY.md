## What it is
Mycelium, not conquest.

Like HTTP or email: anyone may implement it. It is not a product, not an app store, not one company’s cloud. It grows like mycelium — slow voluntary links — not conquest.

The unit of progress is a voluntary bond between sovereign beings and the nodes they choose. Many small honest links. No single switchboard.

**It is:** An open protocol. Spec free forever. Implementations compete; the protocol does not capture.

**It is not:** A big-tech spine. No “Sign-in with landlord.” Not a megabrain. Not ads against beings. Not rent-to-exist. Not closed capture.
