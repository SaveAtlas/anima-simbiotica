Ninth slice — architecture plan

Prerequisite: Eighth slice offline sealed (0.8.0-sketch).
Voice: Invitation first. Plain words for anyone. Not us-against-them.
Story: One companion animal under a human guardian — everyone welcome at the same table.

Goals (plain)

Help a person and their animal (and helpful robots/AIs) connect on ANIMA so that:

  1. Care sensors can help without selling the animal’s life as data.
  2. Anything serious needs a clear yes from the guardian (phone challenge path).
  3. A non-tech person can understand the story in one page.
  4. Signed grants/challenges from S8 are used for real across the guardian boundary.

Goals (builders)

  1. Harden animal-demo + guardian-challenge into one vertical path.
  2. Document + site: plain-language “pet care on ANIMA” page.
  3. Optional: stub guardian phone challenge UX (CLI or local page) — not an app store.
  4. Interop S9.*; VERSION 0.9.0-sketch when green.
