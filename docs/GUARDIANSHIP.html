Guardianship — animals & ecosystems (v0)

Status: Ontology + consent rules for beings that do not speak the protocol alone.
Companions: CONSTITUTION.md definitions, PROTOCOL.md consent, EVENT-AND-CONSENT.md
Rule: Guardianship is accountable, scoped, and challengeable — not ownership cosplay.

1. Why this exists

ANIMA is multi-kingdom. Animals and ecosystems are beings in the constitution sense, but they usually cannot hold keys or click “I agree.”

A Guardian holds consent and care duties in scope. The protocol must record:

  • Who claims to speak
  • For whom
  • For what purposes
  • Until when
  • How the claim can be challenged or succeeded

Economic interest of a human/org must not silently overwrite the dependent being’s recorded interests.

2. GuardianLink

{
  "id": "guard-<id>",
  "type": "GuardianLink",
  "guardian": "did:key:z...human-or-org",
  "dependent": "did:anima:animal:...",
  "dependent_class": "animal | ecosystem | habitat | other",
  "scope": {
    "purposes": ["welfare.monitor", "habitat.sense"],
    "action_classes_allowed": ["robot.sense.*", "memory.write.welfare"],
    "action_classes_forbidden": ["habitat.irreversible.*", "commerce.sell_data"]
  },
  "duties": ["non_maleficence", "inspectable_logs", "best_interest"],
  "status": "active | challenged | revoked | succeeded",
  "issued_at": "2026-09-05T03:00:00Z",
  "expires_at": null,
  "challenge_policy": "any_steward_or_listed_challenger",
  "succession": null,
  "note": ""
}

Dependent identifiers may use did:key when a proxy device holds keys, or a namespaced did:anima:* method later. v0 allows a proxy NodeId under the guardian’s bond with explicit GuardianLink.id on every grant.

3. Consent path

  1. Guardian issues ConsentGrant with guardian_link set.
  2. Actuation / memory grants must not exceed scope.action_classes_allowed.
  3. Anything in action_classes_forbidden → hard deny.
  4. High-stakes habitat/robot acts → fresh_required: true even for guardians.
  5. Challenge → freeze contested grants until resolved (human/legal process outside protocol; protocol records the freeze).

4. Interests record (minimal)

Optional but encouraged:

{
  "type": "DependentInterests",
  "dependent": "did:anima:ecosystem:watershed-x",
  "interests": [
    "minimize_toxin_load",
    "preserve_flow_regime",
    "transparent_sensor_access_for_stewards"
  ],
  "conflict_note": "Guardian economic activity must be flagged when it conflicts with listed interests."
}

This is not a court. It is transparency as immune system.

5. Narrow first verticals (build order)

  1. One companion animal — welfare sensors + guardian phone node
  2. One ecosystem proxy — e.g. watershed or garden plot sensors under a steward group

Do not boil the ocean. Do not claim “nature logged in.”

6. Explicit non-goals

  • Tokenizing animals or ecosystems as speculative assets
  • Silent training on dependent-being data for ads
  • Guardian = owner with unlimited actuation
  • Replacing animal-welfare or environmental law

7. Builder next

  • Schema files + validation that grants reference a GuardianLink when issuer ≠ subject being
  • Demo fixture: guardian + dependent + one allowed sense grant + one forbidden act

Care is a duty recorded in public protocol terms — not a marketing vibe.
