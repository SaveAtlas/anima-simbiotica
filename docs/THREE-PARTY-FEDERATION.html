Three-party federation (S3.3)

Companions: FEDERATION.md, DUAL-PROCESS-BOND.md, THIRD-SLICE.md
Rule: A–B and B–C bonds. No global bus. B is not a megabrain — only a peer with its own grants.

Topology

A ←bond→ B ←bond→ C

A does not automatically gain a bond to C. Transitive introduction may occur via a signed bond.offer hint from B, still requiring A and C consent.

Anti-patterns

  • B forced as mandatory relay for A↔C forever
  • Shared keystore across A/B/C
  • Unsigned introductions

Builder sketch

Three REFNODE_HOME dirs; two dual-bond runs (A-B, B-C); optional introduce event later.

Demo

./run.sh three-party-demo
# equivalent:
python3 -m refnode three-party-demo

Three REFNODE_HOME dirs under data/party-fed-{a,b,c}; A–B and B–C active; A has no bond to C.
