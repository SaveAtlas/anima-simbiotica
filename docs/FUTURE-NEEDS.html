Future needs — Architect foresight (AI + human)

Role: Figure out what AIs need going forward, and what humans will need before they know to ask — so ANIMA holds it before the gap hurts.

North star: Like Earth is Earth, ANIMA is everything.

3. Design rule

If a future AI or human will need it to live together, it belongs inside ANIMA now as a stub, schema, or presence surface — even before the feature is loved.

Outside = optional adapter only.
