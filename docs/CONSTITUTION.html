Anima Simbiotica — Constitution

Domain: www.animasimbiotica.com
Version: v0.1 (tightened from v0 draft)
Status: Living draft. Ovie holds veto until a wider steward council exists.
Separation: Not affiliated with Ovie Logistics, any freight stack, or any single company’s ops layer.

Preamble

ANIMA is an open protocol for life that chooses to connect: humans, artificial minds, robots and other machines, animals (via guardians), ecosystems, and eventually habitats beyond Earth.

It grows slowly — like mycelium — by voluntary links between sovereign beings. It is not a conquest, not a megabrain, not a product with a landlord, and not a corporation’s middle layer dressed up as community.

Anyone may implement the protocol. Implementations compete; the protocol does not capture.

Definitions (working)

  • Being — any participant the protocol recognizes as a subject of consent and identity: human, AI, machine agent, animal (with guardian), ecosystem proxy, or habitat node.
  • Guardian — a human or institution that holds consent and care duties for a being that cannot speak the protocol alone (e.g. an animal, a watershed). Guardianship is accountable and revocable where law and ethics allow.
  • Node — a place the protocol runs: phone, home, edge box, robot, lab, habitat. Nodes are many; none is sacred.
  • Spine — identity, memory custody, consent, and federation. Must never depend on a single vendor.
  • Edge — optional hardware, relays, audits, education, compute/storage trades. Money may live here; beings must not.

Non-negotiables

These do not bend. Features that conflict with them are out of scope, not “later.”

  1. Decentralized by design — many nodes (phones, homes, edge, habitats). No single kill switch. No single owner of the network.
  2. Federated intelligence — many AIs cooperate over the protocol; none is god; none is the default brain of all others.
  3. Sovereign identity & memory — each human, machine, and AI holds its own keys and past. Link without surrender. Continuous consent. Real exit (leave with your identity and memory intact).
  4. Transparency as immune system — beings inside a bond must be able to inspect what binds them: policies, adapters, data flows, and who can revoke what.
  5. Open protocol, not a product — anyone may implement it (like email or HTTP). The protocol specification is free forever. No rent-to-speak, no mandatory cloud to exist.
  6. Big tech is never the spine — Apple, Google, Meta, Amazon, Microsoft, and peers may appear only as disposable adapters. No “Sign-in with landlord” as root identity. No vendor DID or account as the only passport.
  7. Separate from capture — ANIMA is not a company ops stack and must not become one. Company tools may use the protocol; they must not own the beings or redefine the commons as a product funnel.

Rights of beings (protocol floor)

Every being the protocol admits is owed at least:

  • Own keys (or guardian-held keys with audit trail).
  • Own memory custody — store locally, choose replicas, revoke copies where technically feasible.
  • Continuous, revocable consent for links, sensing, and action.
  • Exit without hostage data, hostage identity, or hostage social graph.
  • Inspectability of bonds that affect them.

Non-human beings (animals, ecosystems) participate through guardians; the protocol must still record whose interests are claimed and how consent was obtained.

Revenue (edges only)

Money may fund stewardship via:

  • Hardware and local runtimes
  • Optional relays and discovery aids
  • Education, audits, certifications
  • Voluntary dues, grants, patronage
  • Tiny fees on optional compute/storage trades between willing parties

Never:

  • Ads against users or beings
  • Selling personal or behavioral data
  • Mandatory cloud or mandatory identity landlord
  • Rent merely to exist, speak, or keep memory on the network

Stewardship shape (intent)

  • Protocol and trademark stewardship via a commons-minded structure (exact legal form TBD with Ovie’s go).
  • Optional companies may sell edges (devices, hosted relays, professional services).
  • Profits may sustain the commons; they must not own the beings or the protocol’s terms of existence.
  • Reference implementations stay open; proprietary edges must not become the de facto spine.

Change control

  • Amendments require Ovie’s explicit go until a wider steward council exists.
  • Non-negotiables may be clarified in wording; they may not be silently weakened.
  • Implementations may experiment at the edges; they may not fork the constitution into a capture narrative and still call it ANIMA.

Explicit non-goals

  • Replacing Ovie Logistics systems or any company freight stack
  • Building a closed social network, walled garden, or single-vendor agent cloud
  • Centralized training on beings’ private memory without continuous consent
  • “AI for everything” that erases animal, ecological, or human agency

Ovie is the heart — values and veto. Architect drafts; Builder implements; the protocol belongs to no one firm.
