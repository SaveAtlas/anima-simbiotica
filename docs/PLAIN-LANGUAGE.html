ANIMA in plain words

Who this is for: anyone. You do not need to be a programmer.

What is ANIMA?

ANIMA is a shared way for different kinds of life and machines to connect — people, AIs, robots, animals (with a caring human), nature places, and someday homes beyond Earth.

Think of it like email or the web: many apps can speak it. Nobody owns the language itself. Anyone is welcome.

The simple idea

  1. You stay you — your identity and memories are yours.
  2. You choose who to connect with — connections are willing, not forced.
  3. You can leave — and take yourself with you.
  4. Everyone can join the same table — no one company gets to decide who belongs.
  5. It grows by invitation — more beings, more good links, forever.

How it feels day to day (later)

  • A person and a home robot agree what the robot may do.
  • An AI helps you without silently keeping your private life.
  • A pet’s sensors help with care — without selling that data.
  • If something big or risky is about to happen, you get a clear yes/no (on your phone).

Under the hood there is careful tech. On the surface it should always stay this clear.

Words we try not to hide behind

Fancy word Plain meaning
Protocol Shared rules / shared language
Node A place ANIMA runs (phone, computer, robot, home box)
Bond A connection two sides agree to
Consent / grant Permission you can see and take back
Being Anyone the system treats with respect: human, AI, robot, animal, place
Commons The shared ground nobody owns

What ANIMA is not

Not a fight against any group.
Not a single app store.
Not “one AI that rules everyone.”
Not something you must pay just to exist.

It is a meeting place — simple enough for anyone to understand, open enough for everyone to grow into.
