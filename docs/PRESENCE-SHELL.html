Presence shell — first phone screen

Goal: The everything-layer — like Earth is Earth, ANIMA is everything. Daily life + AIs + robots in one living place on your phone (no store gate). Grow to replace the scatter of social media and the rest — all in one.

First screen (plain)

  • You — still you
  • People / pets / places you care about today
  • Helpers you invited (AIs, robots)
  • Share — pass what matters
  • Ask me — clear yes/no when something serious wants to happen

Feel: warm habitat, Infinitum-like presence — not a settings dump, not a constitution, not Facebook / chat / bank / workplace. One place for daily life + helpers. Something people will actually use; robots and AIs use it too.

Builder notes

site/presence/ or presence/ PWA. Mobile-first. Demo mode fine until refnode wired. See ELEVENTH-SLICE.md, PHONE-PRESENCE.md.

Helpers that plug in (including Infinitum)

The habitat is bigger than any one app. Infinitum Digital Twin (and many more) can sit here as invited helpers / continuing selves:

  • Twin appears as a peer being you invited — memory, voice, presence — under your keys and willing links.
  • Other AIs, robots, home devices, care tools plug in the same way.
  • ANIMA does not become Infinitum; Infinitum does not own ANIMA. Both meet in the open living place.
  • Adapter pattern: product UI to ANIMA events/grants/bonds; store listing optional for the product, never required for the habitat.

Scale check

WeChat proved “one place for daily life” for humans. ANIMA aims past that: humans + AIs + robots, open habitat, all needs inside — bigger than any super-app.
